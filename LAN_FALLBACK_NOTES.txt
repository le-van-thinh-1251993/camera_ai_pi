AI CAMERA - LUU Y KHI MAT INTERNET

1. Hostname LAN phai dung:
   ai-camera.local

2. Endpoint local:
   http://ai-camera.local:8000

3. Dieu kien:
   - ESP32/phone va Raspberry Pi phai cung Wi-Fi/LAN.
   - Pi phai dang chay ai-camera.service.
   - Avahi/mDNS phai dang chay.

4. Khong dung:
   - Khong dung IP cu 192.168.0.94 vi IP co the thay doi.
   - Khong dung ngrok khi mat Internet vi ngrok can Internet.

5. Luong fallback:
   - ESP32 uu tien Supabase.
   - Neu Supabase/Internet loi, ESP32 gui anh qua ai-camera.local.
   - Pi nhan dien local va luu vao data/hybrid/jobs.sqlite3.
   - Khi Internet tro lai, worker dong bo ket qua, anh va audio len Supabase.

6. Cau hinh key:
   - Pi: LAN_API_KEY trong .env.
   - ESP32: LAN_API_KEY_VALUE trong secrets.h.
   - Neu Pi khong co LAN_API_KEY, Pi dung lai API_KEY.

7. Kiem tra tren Pi:
   getent hosts ai-camera.local
   curl http://ai-camera.local:8000/health
   systemctl is-active avahi-daemon
   systemctl is-active ai-camera.service

Khong commit/push thay doi Git neu chua co yeu cau ro rang tu chu project.
